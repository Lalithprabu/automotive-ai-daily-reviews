# Sourcing & honesty disclosure (Day 56)

**Paper:** *Agentic LinkDreamer: Adaptive Neuro-Fuzzy World-Model Decision Making for Intermittent V2X Cooperative Perception*, arXiv [2610.39258](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39258), listed 2026-10-29 (38 pages, 5 figures, 3 tables, submitted to Elsevier per a daily-arXiv digest).

**Read via:** the abstract / architecture summary on the alphaXiv overview page only. arXiv's own abs/html pages were rate-limited for this run, so I did NOT read the full text, tables, ablations or limitations section.

**Paper-sourced (abstract level):** intermittent V2X outages remove collaborator information; causal collaborator memory; relative-motion-conditioned recurrent latent world model predicting missing BEV features; 5-D decision state (prediction uncertainty, perceptual complementarity, memory freshness, communication quality, residual communication budget); differentiable Takagi-Sugeno neuro-fuzzy controller choosing among 5 modes (ego-only, motion-compensated reuse, latent imagination, low-rate temporal-summary refresh, high-rate refresh); objective balancing detection risk, communication, latency, uncertainty and switching; reflection after recovery; V2X-Real V2V split; geometry-derived degradation protocol; reported 90.95 AP on outage frames (IoU threshold not confirmed), 92.9% of the cooperative gap recovered, 0.65 AP below the cooperative upper reference, ~31% less communication than continuous high-rate cooperation.

**This repo's own (NOT the paper's):** the synthetic world (ego-centred 24x24 BEV, 2 m cells, trucks casting shadows, half-parked/half-moving vehicles, static pole clutter, 2-channel features), the ConvGRU world model (43.5K params), the 21K-param centre-heatmap detector, the 9-rule TS controller (360 params), the cost weights, the Markov link process (up/degraded/down), the "AP" (radius-3 m matching on a 2 m grid), and every number in `checkpoints/results.json` and `sweep.json`. The controller is trained from a risk table (detector loss under each mode) with a softmax-weighted expected-cost objective; the paper's training procedure and its switching penalty are not reproduced (hysteresis at inference instead). Reflection-after-recovery is NOT implemented. Yaw / full SE(2) relative motion is not modelled (ego drives straight, integer-cell warp).

**Honest findings (1 seed, 120 held-out episodes x 24 frames, 25.6% of frames are outage frames):**
1. Fusing the collaborator helps: AP(all) ego-only 0.656 -> upper reference 0.814.
2. The paper's headline is NOT reproduced: on outage frames the controller (AP 0.660) is no better than ego-only (0.666); gap recovery is about -4% (paper: 92.9%). Always-reuse (0.695) and always-imagine (0.698) recover ~18-20%; imagination only ties reuse.
3. With the a-priori comm weight 0.10 the controller learned a cheap policy: 70% low-rate refresh + 30% reuse, never imagination or high-rate; 0.18 comm/frame (82% less than continuous high-rate, 70% less than "refresh when up else reuse" at 0.59) but AP(all) 0.708 vs 0.763 for that baseline.
4. Sweeping the comm weight (checkpoints/sweep.json; same test episodes, so treat it as a frontier, not a tuned win): weight 0.05 -> AP(all) 0.739 at 0.39 comm/frame; weight 0 -> 0.739 at 0.44. The simple "refresh when up, else reuse" baseline still has the best AP(all) at 0.59 comm/frame.
5. Demo drive (one scripted seed, not cherry-picked; final running F1): ego-only 0.633, reuse-baseline 0.722, controller 0.699, upper 0.777 -- the controller trails the reuse baseline on this clip while using 0.09 vs 0.27 comm/frame.
6. Likely reasons (untested): in this toy the ego is already fairly good, the world model's error grows quickly with age and it only ties reuse, and the risk-table objective rewards cheapness once the detector loss gaps between modes are small.
