**Selection notes (Day 15):** Re-ran the full four-category sweep for the 2026-09-05 to 2026-09-19 window. Battery search came up empty inside the window — a 15th consecutive miss. V2X and BEV-detection searches surfaced nothing more novel than BeamTransFuser/LDE or Sparse-BEVNet. The trajectory-forecasting search surfaced MM-Future (arXiv:2609.20377, submitted 2026-09-17, 2 days old at run time): the first paper in this series to generate paired action-and-scene proposals jointly (via bidirectional flow matching) and rank them by each proposal's own imagined future, rather than planning a trajectory and treating scene evolution as separate or absent. See `day-15-mm-future/README.md` for the full architecture-to-code mapping, honest training results (including a disclosed generator-vs-scorer performance gap), and sourcing note.
